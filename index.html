Questions

Frequently asked

Still unsure about something? Ask in Discord and staff will help directly.

Is the server pay-to-win?

No. Every rank sold in the store is cosmetic or quality-of-life only — no gear, no currency, no gameplay advantage.

Does it support Bedrock (mobile/console)?

Yes, both Java and Bedrock players connect to the same world through cross-play support.

How do I report a player?

Open a ticket in Discord with their username, what happened, and any evidence like screenshots or clips.

Can I get my ban reviewed?

Yes — every punishment can be appealed. See the appeal steps in the rules section above.
